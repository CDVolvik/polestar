- An option under Questing > Buttons, Mark what they target, off by default:
  the target button and the step key put a skull on the mob and a star on the
  NPC they target, and leave an icon alone that is already there. A press
  that finds neither clears your target, so nothing else gets the icon. Your
  party sees the icons; in a raid only the leader and assistants can set
  them (thanks Turesh).

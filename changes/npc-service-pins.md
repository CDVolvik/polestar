- Map: NPC service and mailbox icons, enabled by default on zone and city
  maps, with category filters in the map's
  tracking menu, tooltips and click-to-navigate. Uses Polestar's bundled Codex
  for services and mailboxes; no QuestieDB addon is needed. Minimap icons are optional.
  On region maps, services in cities and other areas with a detail map share
  a compact group of icons. Totals appear in the tooltip. Click a group to choose
  an NPC and destination;
  entering the area's detail map shows the individual icons
  (thanks RikyZ90).

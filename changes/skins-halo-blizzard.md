- Two new skins, under Options > Appearance > Windows > Skin: Halo (no frame,
  a dark veil with soft edges under the text, the game's gold, Fira Sans) and
  Blizzard (Polestar as one of the game's windows: its frames, red buttons,
  check boxes, font and colours). Polestar's own look stays the default. Every
  window takes the skin, and Style, compact mode and the colours you pick work
  in each. The arrow follows the skin too (Arrow style, "The skin's arrow"):
  a gold dart that turns green within 40 m, then a ring and a check on
  arrival; the 3D and flat arrows can still be picked. The font follows the
  skin unless you pick one (Font, "The skin's font"; Fira Sans joins the list).

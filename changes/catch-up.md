- Open a guide on a character who already did part of the route and it catches
  up: the step count rolls through what is done to where you are. Grey quests
  are now left out by default (Options, "Skip grey quests"), and so are green
  ones behind your progress, unless a later quest needs them. A guide you are
  past hands over to the next one. Item, mount, reputation, profession and
  class guides do every step, and "Do every step" in the guide window's menu
  does the same for a leveling guide.

- At levels 40 and 60, if you have not yet learned riding, Polestar says the
  cost in chat (100 g / 1,000 g, 10% off at Honored with the home faction, and
  your current balance) and points the arrow at your race's riding trainer
  when you can afford it. Warlocks are sent to Strahad Farsan for the Summon
  Felsteed quest chain; Alliance Paladins to Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker for
  the Tome of Nobility chain. Each tier fires once per character. Turn it off
  under Quality of Life > Vendors > Trainers > Riding reminder.
